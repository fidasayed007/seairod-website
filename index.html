FAQ

Common Questions Answered

Whether you're new to international shipping or a seasoned importer — here are answers to the questions we hear most often.

What is a freight forwarder and what do you actually do?
A freight forwarder is an intermediary that organizes the transportation of goods on behalf of importers and exporters. We don't own vessels or aircraft — instead, we leverage relationships with carriers, agents, customs brokers, and truckers to coordinate your entire shipment from pickup to delivery. Think of us as your logistics operations team without the overhead.
What does FCL and LCL mean?
FCL stands for Full Container Load — you book an entire 20ft or 40ft container for your cargo. LCL (Less than Container Load) means your cargo shares container space with other shippers, and you only pay for your portion. FCL is better for large volume and security; LCL is better for smaller loads or when cost efficiency matters more than speed.
How long does ocean freight from India or China to USA take?
Transit times vary by route and port pair. India to USA (East Coast) typically takes 18–28 days. India to USA (West Coast via JNPT–Los Angeles) is around 20–26 days. China to USA West Coast is typically 12–18 days. Add 3–7 days for customs clearance and inland delivery at destination. We'll give you a realistic timeline estimate in your quote.
What documents do I need to import or export goods?
For most international shipments you'll need: a Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading (ocean) or Airway Bill (air), Certificate of Origin (if applicable), and any product-specific certifications. For U.S. imports, your customs broker (which we can coordinate) handles ISF filing and entry documentation. We guide you on every document required for your specific trade lane.
Do you handle customs clearance?
Yes — we coordinate customs documentation support and work with licensed customs brokers at origin and destination to facilitate clearance. We review your commercial invoice, HS codes, and entry details for accuracy. Customs broker fees and duties/taxes are separate and will be outlined transparently in your quote.
What is an NVOCC and why does it matter?
NVOCC stands for Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier. It's a regulated classification under the U.S. Federal Maritime Commission (FMC) that allows freight forwarders to issue their own Bills of Lading and offer independent ocean freight rates. Operating as a licensed NVOCC means Seairod Logistics can provide you with legal protections, competitive rates, and accountability that unlicensed operators cannot.
How quickly can I get a freight quote?
Standard quotes are returned within 24–48 business hours of receiving your shipment details. For time-sensitive situations, contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp and our team will prioritize your request. The more detail you provide in your inquiry (commodity, weight, route, timeline), the faster and more accurate your quote will be.
Can you handle cargo insurance?
Yes. We can coordinate marine cargo insurance coverage for your shipment. Insurance protects your goods against loss, damage, or theft during transit. We strongly recommend it for high-value shipments. Let us know in your quote request and we'll include insurance options in your proposal.
